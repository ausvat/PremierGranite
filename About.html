About Us

Premier Granite and Marble LLC specializes in the professional fabrication and installation of kitchen countertops. With a strong focus on precision, reliability, and quality workmanship, we help homeowners transform their kitchens with expertly installed granite, marble, quartz, and quartzite surfaces.

History of Premier Granite and Marble LLC

With over a decade of experience in the industry, our company has proudly served clients with high-quality granite and marble installation services. We began our journey in Michigan, where we built a strong reputation for craftsmanship and reliability. Several years ago, we relocated to Phoenix, Arizona, and continue to provide the same exceptional level of service to our valued clients. Our team is deeply passionate about what we do, and we take pride in delivering precise workmanship and ensuring that every project is completed to the highest standards.
